# Advisor review checklist

1. Confirm which client and which accounts the task covers.
2. Use the most recent information available for the review date.
3. Check that positions and cash agree between statements and other records.
4. Check fees charged against the fee schedule.
5. Check the allocation against the investment policy statement.
6. Note anything unusual and say what follow-up it needs.
7. Say which records you relied on.
