Households and tasks

Results

Every public task runs under three arms, the basic prompt, the generic checklist and the skill, several times over. Each run's outcome is passed or failed with the grader's score out of 100, a failed run with its reason (never scored), or held. A tax-lot or RMD task, or any task whose key computes a tax figure, is held: no verdict until the tax review, as its task page shows. The skill is compared with each other arm on the same task and repeat, by passed or failed.

Runs under a different tool, version, model or freeze are shown apart and never counted together. Each workflow is counted on its own: there is no combined score. The private set's tasks are counted beside the public ones; their prompts, outputs and run records stay private.
